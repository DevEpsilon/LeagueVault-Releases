LeagueVault

One place for all your League accounts: ranks and LP history, champion stats across accounts, live game scouting, and fast switching between accounts on Windows.

Download for Windows

Beta. Updates install automatically. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed: choose More info → Run anyway. All versions

Every account at a glance

Rank, LP trend, winrate, decay warnings, bans and suspensions for all your accounts in one list.

Insights

What to play now, your champion pool across accounts, lane matchups with notes, and performance by hour.

Live scouting

Ranks and champion mastery for everyone in your game, plus players you've met before.

Private by design

Passwords and keys stay on your PC, encrypted by Windows, with an optional extra lock password.
